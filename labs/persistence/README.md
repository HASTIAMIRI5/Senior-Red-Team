# Exploitation Validation and Access Testing

This lab is intended for controlled validation of access paths, exposure, and defense quality within an approved scope.

## Principles

- Validation should be evidence-driven
- Testing should be aligned to realistic adversary tradecraft
- Impact should be assessed in business terms
- Defensive coverage must be considered throughout

## Typical Focus Areas

- Identity and authentication controls
- Weaknesses in access configuration or trust boundaries
- Opportunity for privilege abuse or control bypass
- Exposure pathways that affect business-critical systems

## Good Validation Questions

- What would a realistic attacker need to reach the target?
- What is the likely blast radius if access is gained?
- How quickly would the environment detect and contain the action?
- Which protective controls are effective, and which are bypassed?

## Reporting Standard

Every finding should clearly answer:

- What happened?
- Why it matters?
- What controls failed or were bypassed?
- What is the likely impact and risk level?
