# Engagement Readiness Checklist

This checklist ensures the engagement is correctly framed, authorized, and operationally prepared before execution begins.

## 1. Authorization and Scope

- [ ] written authorization exists
- [ ] scope and exclusions are clearly agreed
- [ ] in-scope systems and assets are documented
- [ ] out-of-scope boundaries are explicitly marked
- [ ] stakeholder and owner contacts are confirmed

## 2. Asset and Dependency Review

- [ ] critical business systems are identified
- [ ] data sensitivity and handling requirements are noted
- [ ] asset owners and operational owners are mapped
- [ ] identity, network, cloud, and endpoint dependencies are reviewed
- [ ] critical business processes are captured

## 3. Rules of Engagement

- [ ] testing windows are approved
- [ ] escalation paths are established
- [ ] emergency stop and rollback conditions are documented
- [ ] defensive and client touchpoints are identified
- [ ] communication cadence is agreed

## 4. Threat, Risk, and Objective Definition

- [ ] threat scenarios are defined
- [ ] objectives and desired outcomes are clear
- [ ] ATT&CK-aligned tactics are mapped to expected validation work
- [ ] success criteria are measurable and realistic
- [ ] assumptions and limitations are documented

## 5. Operational Security and Evidence Handling

- [ ] only approved tooling and infrastructure are used
- [ ] evidence collection plan exists
- [ ] sensitive content is protected and properly labeled
- [ ] log retention and chain-of-custody practices are reviewed
- [ ] data-handling boundaries are clear

## 6. Defensive Validation Readiness

- [ ] detection coverage is reviewed
- [ ] identity protections and access controls are validated
- [ ] endpoint and network telemetry coverage is assessed
- [ ] gaps or blind spots are documented before active testing
- [ ] response playbooks and escalation contacts are known

## 7. Reporting and Governance

- [ ] report structure and stakeholder audiences are defined
- [ ] severity model is agreed
- [ ] remediation ownership and follow-up process are mapped
- [ ] executive communication requirements are prepared

## 8. Closeout Preparation

- [ ] evidence is organized and validated
- [ ] final technical findings are structured
- [ ] remediation windows and re-validation plans are defined
- [ ] lessons learned are captured for future iterations

## Senior Guidance

If the engagement cannot be explained clearly, validated by policy, and defended with evidence, it is not ready to proceed.
